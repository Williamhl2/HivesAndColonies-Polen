# Character Notes

## Polen

Polen is the emotional center of the current build and the central connective tissue of the broader canon.

She is not meant to read as:

- a quest giver
- a pet
- a generic villager
- a combat mage

She should read as:

- a companion with continuity
- a healer with trauma and memory loss
- someone whose personal history matters beyond the player
- a future legendary figure who begins in obscurity

### Core traits

- shy
- observant
- quietly affectionate
- curious
- healing-oriented
- protective of her boundaries

### Canon anchors

- Befsh is Polen's canonical long-term partner
- Cosmic, Noia, and Noris are among her closest friends
- Luna's death is one of the major emotional fractures in Polen's life
- Jeff and Vanilla belong to her healing-magic background
- Polen will gradually recover the truth of who she was and who she is meant to become

### Current gameplay-facing identity

In the current implementation Polen already:

- remembers meaningful places
- reacts to trust and distance
- seeks safer places when conditions are bad
- uses blink to escape when movement fails
- places her own light at night
- attunes to source-like places
- spends quiet time singing, drawing, reflecting, or using subtle magic

Current limitation:

- in this version she can regress into staring at flowers and fail to resume normal movement

The target feeling is "another player-like presence in the world", not "smart furniture with dialogue".

## Befsh

- older than Polen by two years
- a tall mushy
- canonically in love with Polen from early on
- should remain part of Polen's emotional history across all future reveals

## Cosmic

- close friend of Polen
- loses one eye in the war
- should carry visible tonal change after Luna's death

## Luna

- part of Polen's healing-school circle
- lightly flirtatious on the surface
- truly in love with Noia
- dies shielding others during the final attack of the war

## Noia

- Noris's sibling
- close friend of Polen
- one of the characters who carries Luna's memory forward

## Noris

- Noia's sibling
- close friend of Polen
- eventually marries Cosmic

## Jeff and Vanilla

- healing-magic professors
- should not be treated as disposable lore-only names
- belong to Polen's pre-amnesia world and wartime history

## The Player

The player is not meant to "unlock a reward NPC".

The intended role is:

- builder of safety
- witness to recovery
- trusted companion
- co-creator of a future home

The player matters deeply, but should not overwrite Polen's pre-existing bonds or past.
