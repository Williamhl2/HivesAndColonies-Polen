# Character Notes

## Polen

Polen is the emotional center of the mod.

She is not meant to read as:

- a quest giver
- a pet
- a generic villager
- a combat mage

She should read as a companion with continuity.

### Core traits

- shy
- observant
- curious
- quietly magical
- protective of her boundaries

### Current gameplay-facing identity

In the current implementation Polen already:

- remembers meaningful places
- reacts to trust and distance
- seeks safer places when conditions are bad
- uses blink to escape when movement fails
- places her own light at night
- attunes to source-like places
- spends quiet time singing, drawing, reflecting, or using subtle magic

The target feeling is "another player-like presence in the world", not "smart furniture with dialogue".

That feeling also needs to remain coherent with the wider `Hives & Colonies` ecosystem:

- villages and colony spaces should read as meaningful places
- interiors should feel safer and more personal than generic outdoor cover
- bees, magic, decoration, and home life should reinforce the same identity

### Growth direction

As the story and relationship deepen, Polen should become:

- more comfortable approaching trusted players
- more expressive in animation and gesture
- more anchored to her own residence
- more proactive in colony life
- more visibly joyful without losing her softness

## The Player

The player is not meant to "unlock a reward NPC".

The intended role is:

- builder of safety
- witness to recovery
- trusted companion
- co-creator of a future home

## The Queen and the lost legacy

The royal and historical layers should remain important, but they should not erase the intimate scale of the mod.

The story works best when:

- personal trust comes first
- larger heritage emerges later
- epic meaning grows out of daily companionship
